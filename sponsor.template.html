Why Sponsor

Reach Your Community. Support Democracy.

NEBCD events draw dedicated Democratic activists, local elected officials, and community leaders from across Northeast San Antonio and Bexar County. Sponsorship is a meaningful way to connect your organization with people who show up — at the polls and in the community.

📣

Brand Visibility

Your name and logo featured in event materials, signage, social media posts, and email communications reaching our full membership list.

🤝

Community Credibility

Align your organization with one of the oldest and most active Democratic clubs in Bexar County, founded in 1994.

🗳️

Civic Impact

Sponsorship funds go directly toward voter outreach, candidate support, and grassroots organizing across Northeast San Antonio.
